Questions

Good to know.

What can you track besides ads?

Email campaigns, website and landing page forms, phone calls, CRM deals, online store orders and print marketing such as flyers, mailers and signs, using QR codes, unique links, promo codes and dedicated call tracking numbers.

How do you track print marketing?

Each piece gets its own trackable path: a QR code, a short link, a promo code or a call tracking number. When someone responds, we know which flyer, mailer or sign brought them in.

What does feeding data back mean?

We send your real conversions back to platforms like Meta and Google so their algorithms learn who your best customers are, and we use the same data to build audiences, email segments and retargeting for future campaigns.

Is attribution ever 100% accurate?

No. Some people switch devices, block tracking or hear about you by word of mouth. We report attributed and unattributed results side by side, so you get an honest picture instead of an inflated one.
