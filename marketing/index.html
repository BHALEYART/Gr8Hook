Attribution

Proof, not impressions.

Clicks and impressions don't pay the bills. We connect each campaign to the leads, calls, bookings and sales it produced, so you can see what your ad spend is actually buying.

  • Every ad is tagged. Campaign tracking travels with each click, so the result can be traced back to the ad behind it.
  • Results matched where they happen. Form fills, calls, bookings, CRM deals and online store orders are tied to their campaign.
  • Budget follows the proof. Money moves toward the campaigns producing customers, not just clicks.
  • Honest numbers. Some customers will always switch devices or come back later, so we report attributed and unattributed results side by side.

Questions

Good to know.

Which ad platforms do you run?

Mainly Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google (Search, Performance Max and YouTube). We recommend the mix based on where your customers are and what you're selling.

How do you prove which ads are working?

Every ad is tagged so the lead, call or order it produces carries the campaign it came from. We match those results in your CRM or store, then report attributed results next to the ones we can't trace. No tracking catches every customer, so we show both instead of overstating results.

Do I need to set anything up myself?

No. Your account manager handles the setup: ad accounts, pixels and conversion tracking, call tracking, landing pages and CRM connections. You approve the strategy and creative.

How much does it cost?

Every quote is built around your goals, your market and your ad budget. Set up a quote and we'll send you a clear proposal, usually within one business day.
