Questions

Good to know.

How does the pricing work?

There's a one-time setup cost to get your program ready, then ongoing work is commission-based. Your quote spells out the setup cost and exactly how commission is calculated for your business.

What does the setup cost cover?

Learning your offer and ideal customer, building call and email scripts, preparing target lists, setting up your pipeline and training the team on your business.

Can your team follow up on leads from our ads?

Yes. Fast follow-up is one of the biggest factors in turning a lead into a customer, and our team can work the leads your marketing brings in alongside new outreach.

Will we be able to see what the team is doing?

Yes. Activity, lead status and results are logged in your CRM, and your account manager reviews progress with you regularly.
